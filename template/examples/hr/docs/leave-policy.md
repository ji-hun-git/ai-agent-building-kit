# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 15 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 5 unused days carry over to the next year.

## Sick leave

Employees receive up to 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A doctor's note is needed after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Requesting leave

Request annual leave at least 5 working days in advance. Your manager approves or declines it in the HR system.
